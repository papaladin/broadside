📐 Model Scope

This tool intentionally preserves the original career simulator's abstraction level: travel is distance/speed based, missions are probabilistic, combat is a single coarse resolution, and economy is represented through approximate costs and rewards. Faction identities are added as modifiers on top of that model. It is intended to reveal large statistical disparities, not to reproduce individual engine decisions.

Represented: birth-faction traits, port-faction specialties, reputation focus, trade/smuggle/combat preferences, ship progression, crew/provisions, heat/infamy, travel, approximate encounters, mission income and free trade. Not represented in detail: equipment loadouts, exact encounter state machines, boarding action sequences, individual event choices, actual engine legality, or exact mission generation.

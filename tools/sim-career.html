⚓ Broadside — Career Balance Lab

Statistical career model for overall balance, progression, viability, and faction identity. It is intentionally not engine-backed and does not depend on the archived Career V2 simulator.

⚙ Configuration





📐 Model Contract

This is the authoritative coarse career-balance instrument. It compares six playstyles across all five starting factions. The model simplifies the sequence of play, but quantities that it models are taken from current game data wherever practical. Deliberate approximations are labelled here and in the code: travel is distance/speed based, missions are event-oriented rather than engine-state based, combat resolves in one coarse exchange, cargo is represented through a profit proxy, and detailed event/UI choice trees are omitted; Spanish crew-port restriction is represented explicitly, including dedicated reposition travel when a Spanish captain is below their crew target outside a Spanish port.

Not used here: archived Career V2, engine legality, exact encounter state machines, exact boarding action sequences, detailed equipment loadouts, or detailed crew-biography/trait modelling. Those areas belong to the specialised tools or the main game rather than this balance lab.
